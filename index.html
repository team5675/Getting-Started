HOW WE LEARN

Try. Change. Understand.

Short sessions count. Stop at a lesson’s checkpoint and come back with a question.

Read a little.

New ideas come with small examples and explanations of unfamiliar words.

Make a prediction.

Say what you think will happen. A guess gives you something to compare.

Change one thing.

Run it again. Notice what changed and explain why.

Connect it.

See the same idea in the team’s code, with context for what comes later.

READY WHEN YOU ARE

Start where you are.

New students use stable WPILib 2026 tools. You can learn about SystemCore now without installing alpha packages. At home or with a partner, begin with one working program.

In class, take Lesson 1; tools already installed? Continue with Lesson 2. Swap the keyboard every 10–15 minutes if you work together.

WHEN YOU GET STUCK

Ask a better question.
