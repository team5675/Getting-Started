Seven labs, one project, then three motor labs

Every lab uses the same simulation starter. Each lab is its own step on the route, placed right after the lesson it needs, so you never have to work out when to come back. This page explains the checkpoint folders and lists the labs.

How checkpoints work

The source project starts at Lab 1. Keep your completed edits as you progress. If you need a fresh starting point, stop the simulator and copy the two files from checkpoints/NN: StudentProgram.java goes into src/main/java/frc/robot, and TrainingLine.path goes into src/main/deploy/pathplanner/paths. For example, checkpoint 03 contains the completed behavior from Labs 1–2 with the Lab 3 TODO remaining.

Matching answers/NN files are for comparison after trying. Follow the backup steps before replacing your own work, or make a Git checkpoint if you have learned Git. Checkpoint files outside src do not compile automatically. Every source checkpoint builds; its unfinished behavior is intentional.
