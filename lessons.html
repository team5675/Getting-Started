A beginner’s guide · Team 5675

Find your next small step

Eight guided lessons, with room to pause, repeat, and ask questions.

Choose one lesson at a time

Lessons 1–4 are the first-hours route. Later lessons are for another session, after you can explain a small change from the earlier ones. Each page has its own stopping point. You can reopen any page without losing your place in the code.

Record your current lesson and last working result in learning-notes.txt. The site does not save a progress score or require an account.

First hours

Lesson 1 · 30–60 minutes, plus downloads

Get comfortable with the tools

Find the project, editor, and Terminal.

Lesson 2 · 35–60 minutes

Run your first program

Edit one message and repair a missing semicolon.

Lesson 3 · 30–45 minutes

Give information a name

Read and change variables; distinguish text from a name.

Lesson 4 · 30–45 minutes

Make a decision

Predict both outcomes and test the exact boundary.

Later beginner sessions

Start these after the first four lessons; you do not need to finish them on your first day.

Lesson 5 · 35–50 minutes

Use a method

Define, call, and return from a small method.

Lesson 6 · 30–45 minutes

Repeat a small task

Trace a bounded loop one pass at a time.

Lesson 7 · 45–60 minutes

Build two objects

Create two objects with independent state.

Lesson 8 · 45–75 minutes with a mentor

Start and stop one action

Use a subsystem and command with a timed action.

When you need a reference

  • Help and word guide: restart steps, error messages, punctuation, and definitions.
  • Guided readings from last year’s team: each reading says which lessons prepare you for it.
  • Save and share with Git: use with a mentor when joining the training repository.
